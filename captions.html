Drop in a video or song and get captions for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or CapCut. Most people watch with the sound off, so captioned clips get watched longer. It runs Whisper, a speech-recognition model, right in this browser: your file never leaves your computer and there's nothing to pay.

Click a time to jump there. Fix any words below; the preview and downloads update as you type.

Words

    Good to know

    • Sung lyrics come out rougher than speech, especially with a loud band behind them. Read every line against the lyric sheet before posting. Interviews, show announcements and talking clips come out nearly word-perfect.
    • YouTube: Subtitles → Add language → Upload file → the SRT. CapCut: Captions → Import captions → the SRT, then style them. TikTok and Instagram make their own captions; paste the text in to fix theirs, or burn ours in with CapCut.
    • The first run downloads the model (once; the browser keeps it). A computer is quicker than a phone, and a 3-minute song takes about a minute on Balanced.
    • English only for now.
