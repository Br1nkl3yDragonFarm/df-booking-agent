How a shoot works

A deliberate flight. A better final cut.

We start with your marketing goal — not a promise to fly anywhere. We review the property and plan a route, or an alternative camera move, that protects your space and puts its best features on display.

Then we capture usable sequences, edit them for your audience and deliver the agreed versions, ready for your website, social campaign or listing.

Ask about your location

What to expect

  • Permission from the property owner or authorized manager
  • Clear interior routes, protected belongings and bystander precautions
  • Outdoor flights only when FAA pilot and airspace requirements are met
  • Weather, lighting and space limits may change the shot plan
  • Deliverables, licenses and revisions agreed in writing first

FAA Part 107 rules don’t apply to indoor-only flights, but permission and safety still do. We don’t promise a flight through every doorway or in every weather.

Straight answers

Drone questions.

Can you film indoor tours and outdoor drone video?

Yes, after a location and safety review. A compact HOVERAir flying camera can capture indoor fly-throughs and creative moving shots. Outdoor commercial flights need an FAA-qualified pilot and compliance checks. We plan each route and choose the safest suitable technique — not every location or doorway can be flown.

Will my video be delivered in 8K?

Not necessarily. The HOVERAir X1 PROMAX can capture up to 8K at supported settings, but the final recording and delivery format depends on the equipment, lighting, movement, edit and where the video will be shown. We confirm deliverables before filming.

Is the 3D fly-through on this page real footage?

No. It’s a route plan rendered in 3D in your browser — the same kind of planning we do before a shoot. We’ll replace it with real footage as projects are completed and clients give permission to share them.

What does it cost to travel to my location?

Shoots in Western Kentucky are quoted with the package. Travel outside the area, permits, talent releases and music licensing are quoted separately so you see the full cost before booking.
